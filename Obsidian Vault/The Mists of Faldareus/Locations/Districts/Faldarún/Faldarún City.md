The Capital of the Faldareus Empire.

The city houses millions of residents, and is protected by a magical dome causing all inside to become peaceful and non violent. It is eternally night, though well lit. It is built using circular districts that ascend the mountain. The people within seem blissfully unaware of the rest of the realm's troubles.
It always snows and the running water in this area doesn't harm vampires.
