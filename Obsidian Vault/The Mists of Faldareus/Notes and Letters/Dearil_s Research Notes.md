[[Dearil]]'s Notes

The mist seems to be some form of undead, as it is seen with holy magic that identifies undead. It is sentient, and can be pissed off and amused by events. It rarely interferes with our world however, and seems to be observing rather than acting.

I’ve been listening to rumors across the Faldareus to see what the perception of this phenomena is and the results are mixed. The rumors are as follows:

- It is undead
     Verified by magic
- It is just a natural part of nature
     Claims from druids
- It consumes flesh
     People disappear upon entering it
- It consumes souls
     People who enter come out changed on occasion
- It is a portal
     People who enter it sometime reappear somewhere different

I’ve made contact with some researchers in the area. Notably Rudolph van Richten, Ivan Dilisnya, [[Nurse 'Margaret' Brown|Margaret Brown]], Aurelia Feldrims, Viktra Mordenheim, and Ylissa Von Hresvelg. And with them we’ve been able to discover some more information about the true nature of the mist. It seems to act as both a guardian, and a portal. Mist surrounds the afterlife as some kind of warden, but to keep us in, or keep something out? - More research required

A new discovery might have just been made. We are not the only ones in the mist. In our latest “Swiss Cheese” Theory we have learned that the mist acts like a block of Swiss cheese, where it is ever present, but with pockets inside, some which connect like tunnels, and others being completely isolated. I managed an audience with [[Miriel Faldareus|Miriel]] and she confirmed the other pockets existed, stating:

*“Bad people die too, and need somewhere to go after death. I’ve made many pockets separated by mist to help keep the evil away from the good, though the inner walls aren’t as fool proof as the outer walls”* 

Great news, I’ve been given clearance to travel the Mists. Turns out to safely travel you need some form of catalyst. I’ve also been given documentation from Ivan Dilisnya documenting other groups discovering ways to traverse the mist
