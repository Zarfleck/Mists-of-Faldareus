2 Kinds of Wendigos

- Bestial
A wendigo is created out of cannibalism. When one resorts to cannibalism the spirit of the wendigo is free to enter your body.

Wendigos suffer from eternal hunger, and resort to consuming themselves when no food is present. Whenever they consume another being, their hunger is not satiated, instead the Wendigo merely grows in size.

They are hostile to other wendigos and are intelligent, able to mimic pray and set up traps
