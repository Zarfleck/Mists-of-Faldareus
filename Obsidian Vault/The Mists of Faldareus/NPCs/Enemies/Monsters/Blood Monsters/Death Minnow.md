
These adorable little fish are not as friendly as they first seem WARNING: DO NOT APPROACH MINNOWS IN BLOODY WATERS. These minnows, despite appearing only 2 inches longs, grow to huge sizes when they attack. Within seconds it has been seen growing to the size of small boats, swallowing a target whole, and shrinking back to their tiny size to avoid detection. They also hunt in swarms and know how to focus fire. They are also capable of leaping out of the water, growing large and shrinking before hitting the water again seconds later. They are very fast and rip prey to shreds rapidly. They are often found in wide shallow pools, preferring bloodied waters. Theoretically, the could grow to any size with enough food, though this theory has yet to be proven
