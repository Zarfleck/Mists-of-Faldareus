
These are very lazy, and float peacefully through the ocean… Until their prey is within striking distance. They move extraordinarily quickly (up to 360ft in a turn) and fight in packs, once they sense prey within striking distance. To lure prey within striking distance they let out a beautiful display of mesmerizing colors visible for great distances, and find better success when living in dark or bloodied environments. They conserve their energy only using it in a burst after knowing their prey is within striking distance. They are capable of rocketing out of the water, grappling prey with paralysing stingers, and dragging them into the abyss. In an extreme case, these jellyfish once capsized a small boat in a matter of seconds. They prefer to hunt in warmer waters and can even skip across land and water *(like a skipping stone) to reach more ideal waters (such as bogs, lakes, geysers etc.)*
